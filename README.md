# Making Research Papers Sound Less AI-Written

By Md Sultanul Islam Ovi

A practical guide to clear, evidence-led, and less formulaic research paper writing.

[Read the complete guide](./making-research-papers-sound-less-ai-written.md)

## Use It with an AI Assistant

1. Copy `making-research-papers-sound-less-ai-written.md` into your project.
2. Provide the file to your AI assistant as standing writing guidance.
3. Ask the assistant to apply it as a revision filter without changing verified claims, citations, results, equations, notation, or technical meaning.

Use the guide's Daily AI Instructions for a quick review or its complete prompt library for a detailed revision.
